PeekMusic for Android · Last updated: November 15, 2026

EnglishDeutsch

In short: PeekMusic has no account of its own, no ads, no tracking and sends no crash reports. Your library, history and settings stay on your device. To load music, lyrics and artwork the app talks directly to the services listed below. Only PeekParty (listening together) uses a server of our own.

What stays on your device

Library, playlists, likes, listening and search history, downloads, settings and your sign-ins (YouTube and, if you use them, Spotify, Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Discord) are stored only on your device. You create backups yourself and decide where they go. Your PeekParty friend list (names and friend codes) is also kept on the device so it shows while you are offline. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

PeekParty (listening together)

PeekParty uses our own server listen.peekmusic.org (hosted by Hetzner in Germany) over an encrypted connection (TLS). Only if you use PeekParty, the server stores:

  • a random device token your app creates, stored only as a hash (SHA-256), and your friend code,
  • the name you choose in PeekParty, your friend list and open friend requests (who asked whom, and when),
  • during a party: the room code, the current track, the queue and song suggestions from participants,
  • party invites to friends, in memory only and for at most 10 minutes.

What your friends see: your name, whether you are online and which party you are in. You are online while PeekMusic is open or playing music, and for up to a minute after. Only people who accepted your friend request (or whose request you accepted) see this.

For troubleshooting, the server logs when devices connect, disconnect and sign in to PeekParty friends, with user ID, name and room code, the song ID for song suggestions, and when an invited friend joins a party. The web server in front of it does not log requests, only the IP address when a connection fails. All logs are deleted automatically after 14 days. Parties are deleted when they end. Your friend code, name, friend list and requests stay until you delete them: in the app under PeekParty, Settings, “Delete PeekParty data”, which removes them from the server at once, or by email.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you use the feature) and Art. 6(1)(f) for troubleshooting.

This website

peekmusic.org sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers; the font is hosted here too. The web server does not log requests, only the IP address when a connection fails (deleted after 14 days). If you pick light or dark mode at the top, your browser remembers it locally (localStorage); it never leaves your device.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object. For PeekParty data, write to the email above and include your friend code, or delete them yourself in the app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

PeekParty (gemeinsames Hören)

PeekParty läuft über unseren eigenen Server listen.peekmusic.org (gehostet bei Hetzner in Deutschland). Die Verbindung ist verschlüsselt (TLS). Nur wenn du PeekParty nutzt, speichert der Server:

  • ein zufälliges Geräte-Token, das deine App selbst erzeugt, nur als Hash (SHA-256), und deinen Freundescode,
  • den Namen, den du dir in PeekParty gibst, deine Freundesliste und offene Freundschaftsanfragen (wer wen gefragt hat und wann),
  • während einer Party: Raum-Code, den laufenden Titel, die Warteschlange und Song-Vorschläge der Teilnehmenden,
  • Party-Einladungen an Freunde, nur im Arbeitsspeicher und höchstens 10 Minuten.

Was deine Freunde sehen: deinen Namen, ob du online bist und in welcher Party du bist. Online bist du, solange PeekMusic offen ist oder Musik spielt, und bis zu einer Minute danach. Das sehen nur Leute, die deine Freundschaftsanfrage angenommen haben (oder deren Anfrage du angenommen hast).

Zur Fehlersuche protokolliert der Server, wann sich Geräte verbinden, trennen und bei den PeekParty-Freunden anmelden, mit Nutzer-ID, Name und Raum-Code, bei Song-Vorschlägen auch die Song-ID, und wann ein eingeladener Freund einer Party beitritt. Der Webserver davor speichert keine Zugriffe, nur bei Verbindungsfehlern die IP-Adresse. Alle Protokolle werden nach 14 Tagen automatisch gelöscht. Parties werden gelöscht, wenn sie enden. Freundescode, Name, Freundesliste und Anfragen bleiben, bis du sie löschst: in der App unter PeekParty, Einstellungen, „PeekParty-Daten löschen“, das entfernt sie sofort vom Server, oder per E-Mail.

Rechtsgrundlage ist Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO (du nutzt die Funktion) bzw. lit. f für die Fehlersuche.
