What’s inside

PeekMusic builds on Metrolist and keeps what helps while you listen.

Peek

The line being sung sits right under the cover, with background vocals and a ring through instrumental breaks.

Translation and Latin script

Lyrics in another language get a translation and a romanized line, from the lyrics provider or Google Translate.

PeekParty

Listen in sync with friends. Add them once with a friend code, then join each other’s parties.

Canvas and animated covers

Moving artwork from Spotify Canvas and Apple Music, in up to 1080p, switched on right from the player.

Fewer, clearer settings

Dozens of switches fewer than upstream, and a setup that shows what matters before your first song.

Material You

Colors from your wallpaper or the cover that is playing. Light, dark or pure black.
